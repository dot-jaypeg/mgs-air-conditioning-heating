When should I schedule furnace maintenance before winter?

Early fall is ideal — before the first cold snap, and before every other homeowner in the Valley is calling for the same appointment.

Is a safety inspection really included?

Yes, always. We check the heat exchanger and venting for carbon monoxide risk on every visit — it's not an upsell, it's standard.

How do I find furnace maintenance near me that's actually licensed?

Ask for a contractor's license number and look it up with the California CSLB. Ours is #960470 — you're welcome to verify it before we ever step foot in your home.

How much does a tune-up cost?

Our standard tune-up starts at $89. We'll always confirm the exact price before we start any work, and we offer 0% financing for larger repairs and installs.

What if I'm outside your usual service area?

We're based in Visalia and typically reach Tulare, Kings, Kern, and Fresno counties within about an hour. Call and we'll let you know.
