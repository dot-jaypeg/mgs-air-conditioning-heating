The Difference

What a proper install actually looks like.

Most HVAC headaches trace back to shortcuts taken during the last install. Here's what we do differently.

The Corner-Cutting Way

  • Unit sized off the old one, not your actual home
  • Old, leaky ductwork reused as-is
  • Refrigerant charge eyeballed, not measured
  • Whoever's on shift shows up, salesperson long gone

The MG's Standard

  • System sized to your home's actual load
  • Ductwork inspected, sealed, or replaced
  • Refrigerant charge measured to manufacturer spec
  • Miguel reviews the job before we call it done
