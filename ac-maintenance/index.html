How often should I schedule air conditioner maintenance?

Most homes in the Valley do well with one ac maintenance visit each spring, before the heat really sets in. Systems that run hard all summer — or homes with pets and heavy dust — can benefit from a second check in the fall.

What's included in an ac maintenance visit?

We clean the coils, check refrigerant levels, inspect electrical connections, test the capacitor and contactor, check airflow, and calibrate the thermostat — then walk you through what we found.

Do you offer ac maintenance near me if I'm outside Visalia?

We're based in Visalia and regularly serve Tulare, Kings, Kern, and Fresno counties — roughly a 1-hour radius. Give us a call and we'll let you know if you're in range.

Are your ac maintenance contractors licensed?

Yes — California Contractor License #960470, fully insured and bonded, and every job gets a final walkthrough from the owner.

Can maintenance actually lower my energy bill?

A neglected system has to work harder to hit the same temperature, which shows up on your bill. Clean coils, correct refrigerant charge, and good airflow are the biggest factors — maintenance addresses all three.
